About

What this site is, where its lessons come from, and how the code is licensed.

What this site is

English Resources is a curated index of third-party English courses: video, audio, grammar, and tests. It hosts no lessons itself. Opening a lesson takes you to the source — YouTube, iVoox, RTVE, or another course site — in one tap.

Sources and ownership

All videos, audio, and external course materials belong to their respective owners. This site does not claim ownership of those materials. Every link names its destination (for example, “Watch on YouTube” or “Listen on iVoox”), and embedded players are the official YouTube, iVoox, Libsyn, or Spotify embeds with a direct link alongside, so the lesson still opens if the embed fails.

Some older course sites only offer http:// addresses. Those links are published as recorded in the source notes and are labelled on the page.

Site code license

This site’s own code and pages are released into the public domain under the Unlicense. That license covers this site only — linked third-party materials remain the property of their owners.

Privacy

This site has no accounts, forms, or analytics and collects no data. YouTube, iVoox, Libsyn, and Spotify embeds are third-party content and may set cookies under their own policies.
